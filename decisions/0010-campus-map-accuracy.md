## What's being decided

The whole point is helping students find their way around the real Rose Hill. How closely should the game map match the real one?

## Options

### A. "Recognizable, not to scale" (recommended)
- Real buildings sit in the right *relative* positions (what's north of what, and which paths connect), but distances are compressed so walking stays quick.
- **Pros:**
  - Players build a real mental map.
  - The game stays fast and fun.
  - This is how Pokémon towns and theme-park maps work.
- **Cons:**
  - Needs a campus map reference.
  - Someone who knows Rose Hill must check the layout.

### B. Illustrative (like the current prototype)
- Buildings appear, but the layout is made up for gameplay.
- **Pros:** Easiest to build, with no research.
- **Cons:** Teaches the wrong spatial layout, which works against the onboarding goal.

### C. Geographically accurate to scale
- **Pros:** Maximum realism.
- **Cons:**
  - Rose Hill is about 85 acres. At this scale walking takes minutes of empty grass, and the map gets very large.
  - Much more art.

## My recommendation

**Option A.** "Where is this building, and how do I get there?" is one of the main things students should take away, and a made-up layout teaches them the wrong answer. Next steps if approved:
- Pick the 8–12 buildings that matter most (see D-0001 and D-0003).
- Sketch their relative positions from the official campus map.
- Rebuild the Rose Hill maps from that sketch.

If you can share or point me to a campus map you trust, I'll draft a layout sketch for you to check.
