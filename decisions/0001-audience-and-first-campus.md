## What's being decided

Who is the player, and which campus do we build first? This decides which buildings, offices and tips go into the game, and how long the content work takes. Fordham has three campuses: Rose Hill (Bronx), Lincoln Center (Manhattan) and Westchester.

## Options

### A. First-year undergraduates, Rose Hill only (recommended)
- **Pros:**
  - The biggest group that is new to campus at the same time.
  - Rose Hill is the main residential campus.
  - A single map keeps the content small enough to finish and check properly.
- **Cons:**
  - Lincoln Center, transfer and grad students aren't served at first.

### B. All new students (first-years, transfers, grad), Rose Hill only
- **Pros:** More useful to more people from day one.
- **Cons:**
  - The groups need different things. Grad students don't care about residence halls; transfers already know how college works.
  - Content becomes generic, or splits into several quest lines early.

### C. First-years at both Rose Hill and Lincoln Center from the start
- **Pros:** Serves both undergraduate campuses.
- **Cons:**
  - Roughly doubles the map, art and content work before anything ships.
  - Lincoln Center is a vertical, urban campus that needs different art (city streets, a high-rise).

## My recommendation

**Option A.** One audience and one campus let us make one thing that's actually good and test it with real students. Campus facts live in data files (D-0006), so adding Lincoln Center or a transfer-student quest line later is mostly content work, not engine work.
