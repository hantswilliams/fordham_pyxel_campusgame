## Are there two?

**Yes, but only one of them is a field of its own.**

- **Bahoshy Field** (softball) is its own diamond beside the tennis courts. It has an all-dirt infield, a red-brown warning track and a paved plaza behind home plate.
- **Houlihan Park** (baseball) is not a separate field. Wikipedia calls it "the baseball field within Jack Coffey Field". In the satellite view, the diamond's bases and mound are dirt cut-outs in Jack Coffey's FieldTurf at the south end. The outfield runs north over the football field, towards Moglia Stadium.

The game had drawn Houlihan Park as a separate field south of Jack Coffey, which was wrong.

- **Walkway:** the one that ran between the two "fields" is gone.
- **Cards:**
