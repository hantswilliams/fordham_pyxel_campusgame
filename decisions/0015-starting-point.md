## What's being decided

The first 30 seconds set the tone. Where does a new student appear when the game starts?

## Options

### A. The Main Entrance at Matteo Ricci Circle (recommended)
- The front gate on Southern Boulevard. An Orientation Leader greets you, and Keating Hall and Edwards Parade are right there.
- **Pros:**
  - The official front door.
  - Seconds from the campus hub, so the first checklist step happens straight away.
  - Easy to explain: "This is the main gate. Everything starts here."
- **Cons:** Many students actually arrive by train or subway, through the southwest gate.

### B. The Third / Webster Avenue Gate (from Metro-North or the subway)
- **Pros:** Realistic for commuters and visitors. Teaches the transit route from the start.
- **Cons:**
  - Starts in a corner, a long walk from Edwards Parade.
  - A slower opening.

### C. Outside a residence hall ("move-in day")
- **Pros:** A nice story, and feels personal.
- **Cons:**
  - Students live in different halls, so any choice is arbitrary.
  - Starts deep in the residential area, away from the hub.

## My recommendation

**Option A.** A strong opening needs the player in the middle of things fast, and the main gate is next to Edwards Parade. The commuter route is still taught by checklist step 6 ("learn how to get off campus"), which sends players to the Webster Avenue Gate later.
