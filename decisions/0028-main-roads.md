## How I compared them

I lined up the street map with the game map using seven places both share: the Walsh Library, University Church, McShane, the gym, Parking Area A, Edwards Parade and the Webster Avenue gate. The game map is the real campus turned about 35° and squeezed, and every landmark lined up to within about four tiles. I then marked which tiles fall on the gray roads.

## What already matched

- Bellarmine Road from the church down to McShane.
- Constitution Row from McShane to Parking Area A.
- Xavier Way to the Main Entrance.
- Rose Hill Manor Drive down the middle.
- Clavius Way / East 191st Street along the south.
- The Old Elm Road loop round Martyrs' Court (D-0027).
