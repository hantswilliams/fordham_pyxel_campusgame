## What's being decided

Rose Hill has more than 50 buildings on the map. Which ones get a **Campus Guide entry**, and which make up the **First Week Checklist** (the main quest)? All locations below come from the official map (see `research/rose-hill-map-notes.md`). The *descriptions* still need Fordham staff review.

## Options

### A. A 7-step checklist and about 20 Campus Guide entries (recommended)

**First Week Checklist:**

| # | Step | Where | Badge |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Meet your Orientation Leader | Edwards Parade | Community |
| 2 | Grab a meal | McShane Campus Center | Dining |
| 3 | Find a place to study | William D. Walsh Family Library | Academics |
| 4 | Know where to go if you're sick or stressed | O'Hare Hall (Health Center and Counseling Center) | Wellness |
| 5 | Find Public Safety | Thebaud Hall | Wellness |
| 6 | Learn how to get off campus | A Ram Van shuttle stop, and the Third / Webster Avenue Gate for Metro-North | Community |
| 7 | Find your classes | Visit 2 of Keating, Dealy, Hughes, Freeman | Academics |

**Campus Guide (about 20 entries):**
- **Everything above:** the checklist places.
- **Other buildings:**
  - Keating, Dealy, Hughes and Freeman Halls
  - Cunniffe House
  - Office of Undergraduate Admission
  - University Church
  - Rose Hill Gymnasium
  - The Fordham Store
- **Landmarks:**
  - Ram Statue
  - Victory Bell
  - Seismic Observatory
  - 9/11 Memorial Garden
  - Martyrs' Lawn
  - Moglia Stadium and Jack Coffey Field
- **Residence halls:** one entry per *cluster*, not per hall:
  - West: Martyrs' Court, Alumni Court, Queen's Court and neighbors
  - Faber and Loyola
  - O'Hare and Tierney
  - Finlay and Walsh

**Career badge:** career services isn't on the map. I'll leave a slot for it and add it once Fordham tells us where it is.

- **Pros:**
  - Covers the questions new students actually have: food, study, health, safety, transport, classes.
  - Small enough to write well and check.
- **Cons:** Some offices students need (ID cards, residential life, advising) are missing until Fordham supplies their locations.

### B. Smaller: a 5-step checklist and about 10 entries
- **Pros:** Ships sooner, with less to verify.
- **Cons:** Thin. A player finishes in about 5 minutes and learns less.

### C. Everything: every academic building and every residence hall
- **Pros:** Complete.
- **Cons:**
  - 50+ entries to write and check.
  - Most are low-value ("Collins Hall: classrooms").
  - The checklist loses focus.

## My recommendation

**Option A.** Each checklist step answers a real first-week question, and the order sends the player on a loop around the whole campus (center → north → southwest → south → east). The checklist also needs **Fordham input** on:
1. Where career services, the ID card office and residential life are.
2. A quick fact-check of every description before launch.
