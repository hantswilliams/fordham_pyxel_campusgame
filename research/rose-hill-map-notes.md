# Rose Hill campus: notes from the official map

Source: `research/Rose-Hill-Campus-Map.pdf` (Fordham's Rose Hill Campus Map). I read these notes off the map on 2026-10-09. They are the reference for building the game map (D-0010, D-0013) and for content (D-0014).

**What the map does *not* tell us:** what's inside most buildings, office hours, and where career services, the ID card office, residential life, advising and the dean of students are. **Those need Fordham staff input before we write dialogue about them.**

## Orientation

- **North and northeast:** Southern (Kazimiroff) Boulevard, with the New York Botanical Garden across it.
- **South:** Fordham Road and East 191st Street.
- **West:** Webster Avenue and the Metro-North railway tracks.
- **Southeast:** Belmont and Hughes Avenues, with the Bronx Zoo beyond.
- **The center of gravity is Edwards Parade.** It's a large oval lawn east of the middle of campus.

## Zones (as they'd work in the game)

### 1. Central: Edwards Parade (hub)
- **Edwards Parade:** the oval lawn.
  - West side: Rose Hill Manor Drive.
  - North side: Constitution Row.
- **Keating Hall:** east side of the Parade, academic, has a **dining** icon. Its "Entrance" faces north toward Constitution Row.
- **Freeman Hall:** south side, academic, next to the **Seismic Observatory**.
- **Spellman:** east of Keating, classed as "other building."

### 2. Central-west: academic core
- **Academic buildings:**
  - **Hughes Hall**, beside the **Carolyn Dursi Cunniffe Fountain** and the **Ram Statue**.
  - **Dealy Hall**, with a **dining** icon and a shuttle stop out front.
  - **Collins Hall**
- **Cunniffe House:** "other building."
- **Alpha House**
- **Statues:** St. Ignatius, and Archbishop Hughes (the second sits between Martyrs' Lawn and Cunniffe House).
- **University Church:** northwest, beside the cemetery and Bellarmine Road.

### 3. East: O'Hare area
- **O'Hare Hall:** residence hall. The map shows a **Health Center**, **The Fordham Store**, and **dining** here, and the restroom list mentions a **Counseling Center** in O'Hare.
- **Tierney Hall:** residence hall.
- **Fordham Regional Parking Facility:** RamVan pick-up and drop-off. **Parking Area A** is nearby.
- **Southeast corner:** Canisius, Faculty Memorial Hall and Cambreleng Row.

### 4. West: residential
- **Residence halls:**
  - Loschert
  - Alumni Court South
  - Bishop's, St. John's and St. Robert's
  - Queen's Court (has **dining**)
  - Martyrs' Court
  - Conley and Salice
  - Campbell (dining nearby)
- **Faber and Loyola:** residence halls a little further east.
- **Alumni House** (Rodrigue's)
- **Martyrs' Lawn:** a large lawn.
- **Roads:** Edgar Allan Poeway, Mill River Road and Archer Drive.

### 5. Southwest: library and admissions
- **William D. Walsh Family Library**, on **Walsh Lawn**.
- **Larkin Hall:** academic.
- **Office of Undergraduate Admission:** shown in maroon on the map.
- **Thebaud Hall:** academic, with the **Public Safety Office**.
- **9/11 Memorial Garden**
- **Road:** Old Elm Road.

### 6. South: residential
- **Residence halls:** Finlay and Walsh Hall. Walsh Hall is a residence hall, *not* the library.
- **Mulcahy Hall:** academic.
- **Road:** Clavius Way, along the south edge.

### 7. North: athletics
- **McShane Campus Center:** **dining**, two icons. This is the student center (the old McGinley Center name doesn't appear on this map).
- **Rose Hill Gymnasium**, the **Victory Bell**, and the Lombardi Memorial Center.
- **Moglia Stadium**, **Jack Coffey Field** and **Houlihan Park**.
- **Murphy Field**, **Bahoshy Field**, and the Hawthorn/Rooney tennis courts with the Tennis House.

## Roads vs. walkways (see decisions/0016)

The official map draws named roads as wider white bands than walkways:
- Xavier Way and Matteo Ricci Circle
- Constitution Row
- Bellarmine Road
- Rose Hill Manor Drive
- Edgar Allan Poeway
- Mill River Road
- Old Elm Road
- Clavius Way

Ram Van stops sit on Rose Hill Manor Drive and Edgar Allan Poeway, and at the Alumni Court circle. The map labels the Main Entrance "Vehicular Entrance." It labels the Bathgate Avenue and Clavius Way / Belmont gates "Pedestrian Entrance."

**Label conflict:** Google Maps shows "Old Elm Rd" near Martyrs' Court and between Collins Hall and Alpha House. The official map puts Old Elm Road in the southwest, across Walsh Lawn. The game follows the official map. **Ask Fordham.**
