# Keating Hall: notes

**Status:** in review (v1, house style). Fourth drawn building (decisions/0020, 0021).

## Sources

- `research/images/Keating_Hall,_2014_crop.png`: the front from Edwards Parade. It shows:
  - Collegiate Gothic in warm tan fieldstone, banded with pale limestone
  - the central tower: corner turrets, battlements, a tall tracery window, and a carved crest
  - a tracery window over the pointed-arch entrance, with wide stone steps down to the lawn
  - symmetrical wings with crenellated parapets and corner turrets: two storeys of rectangular windows over pointed-arch windows on the ground floor
  - hedges and a low wooden rail fence along Edwards Parade
- `research/images/keating_terrace-1024x490.jpg`: the entrance at commencement, with maroon banners hanging beside it.
- `research/images/keating_insta.jpg`: the same front in autumn.
- `research/images/keating_hall_facing_east.png`: Keating seen across Edwards Parade.

We don't know who owns these photos, so they're tracing references only and are never shipped in the game.

## What the drawings show

- `map.png` (160 × 122 px): Keating in the house style.
  - The footprint is 10 × 7 tiles, bigger than the old 8 × 7 tile version, since it's one of the biggest buildings on campus. It's centred on the same entrance.
  - The flat roof of the wings has a crenellated parapet and turrets at the corners.
  - The front wall has rectangular windows over pointed-arch windows, with buttresses.
  - The banded tower rises from the ground through the roofline, 10 px above the footprint. It has turret pinnacles, the great tracery window and the crest.
  - The pointed-arch entrance sits under a smaller tracery window, with maroon banners on either side.
- `card.png` (228 × 88 px): the classic view from Edwards Parade: the tower, both wings, the banners, the steps, the hedges and rail fence, and autumn trees.
- **The game adds** the plaque (KEATING HALL) and the mats.

## Simplifications (open for review)

- **The entrance faces south in the game.** The real entrance faces west, up the steps from Edwards Parade; the card says so under **IN REAL LIFE**.
- **Banners.** The maroon banners are up for commencement in the photo and aren't always there. They're kept as Keating's signature; easy to remove if you'd rather not.
- **Wing windows** are simplified to a regular grid.
