# Walsh Library: notes

**Status:** in review (first drafts, v1). Pilot building for decisions/0020.

## Sources

- `research/images/Walsh-1078_lookingSouth.jpg`: the north façade from Walsh Lawn, looking south. Light gray stone, square piers whose caps rise above the roofline, a row of tall glass arches (the entrances), windows above, a dark glass top floor, and the slatted tower behind the middle. **We don't know who owns this photo**, so it's a tracing reference only and is never shipped in the game.
- `research/images/zoomin_southwest.png`: the official map's outline. A narrow block at the northwest, a main body, and a long wing to the east with angled ends. The entrances face Walsh Lawn.

## What the drawings show

- `map.png` (160 × 148 px): the whole building from above. A two-storey stone façade runs along the north edge of the tower block and the wing, with one glass arch per tile; every arch is an entrance. Behind it is a flat roof with a stone parapet, skylights and rooftop units, and the slatted tower standing on the roof with its shadow. The SE end is angled, and the drawing sits 4 px higher than the footprint so the pier caps rise above the roofline.
- `card.png` (228 × 88 px): the view from Walsh Lawn, as in the photo. It has the stone wings, the centre with seven tall glass arches, the tower behind, cypresses, lamps, and a student for scale.

## Simplifications (open for review)

- **Outline.** The real building's lawn side is a **curved** façade facing northeast. On the 16 px grid it's drawn as two straight steps (tower block, then wing), as in the approved mockup A. A stepped or curved façade could come later if it reads better.
- **Façade on the north edge.** The camera looks north, so a north-facing front can't really be seen from above. The façade is drawn upright on the north edge to say "this side is the front" (see D-0019 and D-0020).
- **The domed building** behind the library in the photo is off campus, so it's left out of the card picture.

## Open questions

- Which arches are real, usable entrances? The game currently treats every arch as a way in.
- Are the tower's position and height right?
- Should the card picture show the curve of the façade more strongly?
