# Carolyn Dursi Cunniffe Fountain: notes

**Status:** in review (v1). The first drawn **landmark**: no entrance, and the sign object on its cell opens its card (decisions/0022).

## Sources

- `research/images/Carolyn_Dursi_Cunniffe_fountain,_Fordham.png`: a tall, tiered dark-bronze fountain in a round stone basin, on a paved plaza in front of Cunniffe House, with clipped hedges, yellow mums, benches and lamp bollards. We don't know who owns this photo, so it's a tracing reference only.

## What the drawings show

- `map.png` (32 × 46 px, a 2 × 2-tile basin): the paving round the basin, the stone rim and water, and the two bronze tiers with falling water. The upper tier and finial rise 14 px above the basin.
- `card.png`: the fountain in front of Cunniffe's door and pink wing, with hedges, mums, benches and the paved plaza.
- **On the map** it sits at the exact centre of the plaza between Cunniffe House, Hughes Hall and Dealy Hall.
