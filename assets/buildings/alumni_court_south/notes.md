# Alumni Court South: notes

**Status:** in review (v1). Part of the residence-hall batch, decisions/0024. It's a drawn building on the footprint it already had, entrance facing south.

## Sources

Found online for this batch (decisions/0024). Photos are tracing references only and are never shipped in the game.

## What the drawing shows

3 × 8 tiles. Loschert's twin: the same brick and gabled glass bay. Long and narrow, so the roof shows more than the front. The card shows a Ram Van at the stop.
