# Conley & Salice Halls: notes

**Status:** in review (v1). Part of the residence-hall batch, decisions/0024. It's a drawn building on the footprint it already had, entrance facing south.

## What the drawing shows

3 × 9 tiles. Charcoal brick with a glass stair tower and a cast-stone base, like Campbell Hall; a line on the roof marks where Conley meets Salice. The card shows both halls.
