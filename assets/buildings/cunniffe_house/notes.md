# Cunniffe House: notes

**Status:** in review (v1, house style). Drawn building for decisions/0022 (the fountain quad).

## Sources

- `research/images/Cunniffe_House,_2017.jpg`: the front.
  - A three-storey centre block in gray stucco, with cream stone window surrounds, a white top storey and a dentil cornice.
  - A white octagonal cupola with a cross on the roof.
  - The arched front door has an iron grille, with lanterns either side.
  - Lower pink-brick wings with white trim and arched ground-floor windows.
- `research/images/Carolyn_Dursi_Cunniffe_fountain,_Fordham.png`: the fountain right in front of the door. The "Administration" sign is there too.

We don't know who owns these photos, so they're tracing references only.

## What the drawings show

- `map.png` (80 × 76 px, 5 × 4 tiles): the wings' low roofs and pink-brick walls, and the taller gray centre block. The cupola and cross rise 12 px above the footprint. The arched iron door with lanterns opens south onto the paved strip that joins the fountain plaza.
- **On the map** it stands on the **left (west) of the plaza**, centred between Hughes Hall (north) and Dealy Hall (south), with the fountain in the middle.
- `card.png`: the front as in the photo, with hedges, a lamp post and bare trees.
- **The game adds** the plaque (CUNNIFFE) and the mats.

## Simplifications and questions

- **Entrance.** The real front door faces east, onto the fountain; in the game it faces south onto the same plaza. The card says so under **IN REAL LIFE**.
- **Facts to confirm with Fordham:** that it's the original Rose Hill manor house and the oldest building on campus, and that it holds administration offices.
- **New colors.** The pink brick uses the two colors added in D-0022.
