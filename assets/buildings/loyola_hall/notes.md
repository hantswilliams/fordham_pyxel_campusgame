# Loyola Hall: notes

**Status:** in review (v1). Part of the residence-hall batch, decisions/0024. It's a drawn building on the footprint it already had, entrance facing south.

## What the drawing shows

7 × 4 tiles. Gray Collegiate Gothic stone with battlements and a taller central entrance bay: tracery window over a pointed-arch door.
